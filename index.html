    How to print it
    • 56 mm across, 8.6 mm tall. About 30 minutes and 14 g of PLA on a Bambu P1S.
    • Two colours (AMS): open the 3MF in Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer. The body is filament 1 and the logo is filament 2; there is one colour change per plate.
    • One colour, swap by hand: print the one-colour STL and add a filament change at 8.2 mm.
    • 0.2 mm layers, no supports, no brim. Copy it nine times on a plate; it is still one colour change.
    • When it is done, twist each ring gently to free it. If one sticks at the bottom, run a craft knife round that gap, or reprint on Loose.
